🌿 Our Story

Cooking With Passion,
Serving With Heart

Founded in 2012 by Chef Marco Aldini and sommelier Elena Rossi, Savoria Bistro was born from a simple idea — that every meal should feel like a celebration of local harvest and human connection.

We partner directly with certified regional farms within 50 miles, selecting peak produce every morning. Whether you join us for a quiet dinner or pre-order for seamless pickup, expect warmth in every detail.

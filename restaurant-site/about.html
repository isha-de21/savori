Our Story & Heritage

Rooted in Passion, Crafted with Heart

From a humble hearth in 2014 to a celebrated sanctuary for European gastronomy.

Craftsmanship

A Decade of Heartfelt Hospitality

Founded in 2014 by culinary partners Chef Marco Rossi and Elena Rossi, Savoria Bistro was born from a desire to bring old-world hearthside cooking into contemporary dining. What started as a cozy 24-seat boutique has blossomed into the valley's favorite destination for seasonal culinary celebrations.

Our kitchen honors the authentic craft: making fresh pasta every morning from organic stone-ground flour, slow-smoking meats over aromatic cherrywood, and sourcing heritage greens directly from organic growers within fifty miles.

The Ambience: Step into an intimate sanctuary filled with the gentle crackle of our open wood-fired hearth, soft acoustic jazz melodies, and natural reclaimed oak dining tables bathed in golden candle glow.

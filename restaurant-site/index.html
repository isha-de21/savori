Our Story

More Than a Restaurant

Founded in 2012 by Chef Marco Aldini and sommelier Elena Rossi, Savoria Bistro was born from a dream — to create a space where food is a celebration of local land and human connection.

Tucked in the heart of Culinary District, our 60-seat bistro has become a community gathering place. We host private dinners, wine-pairing evenings, and seasonal tasting menus that change with every harvest cycle.
